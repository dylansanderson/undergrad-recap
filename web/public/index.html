    About this series

    Computer Engineering, Re-Taught re-teaches the core of a computer engineering bachelor's degree, one course at a time, in a single narrated lecture series: math and physics, programming and data structures, circuits and electronics, digital logic and processor design, embedded systems, and senior design.

    It is written for anyone who wants a second explanation: current students, graduates refreshing what didn't stick, and curious people starting from scratch. Every lecture assumes nothing was retained and works through concrete examples.

    Ask the narrator

    Pause at any moment and type or speak a question. The narrator knows which slide is on screen and what was just said, and answers in the same voice, with code or a whiteboard drawing when that helps. Answers are generated by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, and can contain mistakes: check anything important against a textbook.

    To keep the site free, questions are limited per visitor per hour and per day. Your question and the current slide are sent to Anthropic to write the answer. Questions are logged so the series can be improved; please don't type personal information.

    Independence

    This is an independent educational project. It follows the course sequence of the computer engineering program at California State University, Fullerton, but it is not affiliated with, produced by, or endorsed by the university.
